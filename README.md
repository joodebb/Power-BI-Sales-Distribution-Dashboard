Sales Distribution Dashboard | Power BI
Project Overview
The Sales Distribution Dashboard is an interactive Business Intelligence solution developed using Microsoft Power BI to analyse sales performance, monitor revenue trends and provide insights into customer, product and regional sales distribution.
The dashboard transforms sales data into meaningful visual insights, enabling stakeholders to monitor Key Performance Indicators (KPIs), compare actual sales against targets, identify top-performing customers and products, and evaluate sales performance across different regions and time periods.
The solution provides a consolidated view of sales performance through interactive visualisations, dynamic filters and geographical analysis, supporting data-driven decision-making and improved business performance monitoring.
Key Features & Functionality
•	Total Sales KPI: Provides a high-level overview of overall sales performance.
•	Monthly Sales Analysis: Compares actual sales against sales targets to identify performance trends and variances.
•	Top 3 Customers: Identifies the highest-value customers based on actual sales contribution.
•	Regional Sales Distribution: Uses a doughnut chart to analyse sales contribution across different geographical regions.
•	Product Performance Analysis: Highlights top-performing products based on sales revenue.
•	Weekly Sales Trends: Visualises weekly sales patterns to identify fluctuations and performance trends over time.
•	Geographical Sales Analysis: Uses map visualisation to demonstrate sales distribution across different countries.
•	Interactive Month Slicer: Enables users to filter and analyse sales performance by individual months.
•	Regional Filtering: Allows users to explore sales performance across specific regions.
Business Value
The dashboard provides business stakeholders with a centralised view of sales performance, helping them to:
•	Monitor overall revenue and track progress against sales targets.
•	Identify high-value customers and understand customer contribution.
•	Evaluate product performance and identify leading revenue generators.
•	Compare regional sales performance and geographical distribution.
•	Identify weekly and monthly sales trends to support forecasting and planning.
•	Make informed decisions using interactive, data-driven insights.
